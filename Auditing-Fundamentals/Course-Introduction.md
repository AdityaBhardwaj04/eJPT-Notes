## Overview

This course focuses on **security auditing from a penetration tester's perspective** — not on how to perform a full security audit, but on understanding what audits are, how they differ from pen tests, and how audit findings shape the scope and objectives of a penetration test.

**Instructor:** Alexis Ahmed — Senior Penetration Tester at HackerSploit, Red Team Instructor at INE.

---

## Why This Matters for Penetration Testers

Many organizations use a **security audit as the basis for commissioning a penetration test**. The audit findings define:
- What areas to test
- What compliance standards must be satisfied
- What has already been remediated (and needs to be verified)

If a company processes credit card data and must comply with **PCI DSS**, you need to tailor your pen test to meet those requirements — not just find vulnerabilities.

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of cybersecurity concepts
- Familiarity with the penetration testing lifecycle (recon → enumeration → exploitation → reporting)
- Basic awareness of compliance standards (PCI DSS, ISO 27001) — just awareness, not deep knowledge

---

## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand the role of security auditing** within an organisation
2. **Differentiate** between security audits, vulnerability assessments, and penetration tests
3. **Learn the security auditing process** — the steps in a comprehensive audit
4. **Assess compliance requirements** — how to evaluate adherence to regulatory standards
5. **Apply security auditing techniques** in the context of penetration testing
6. **Integrate audits with penetration testing** — how to perform a pen test when the scope is derived from an audit report
