## Prerequisites

- **Networking fundamentals** — OSI model, TCP, UDP → [[Networking-Fundamentals-OSI]] | [[Transport-Layer-TCP-UDP]]
- **Protocol familiarity** — FTP, SSH, SMTP, etc.
- **Pentesting lifecycle** — understanding where enumeration fits → [[Active-Information-Gathering]]
- **Nmap** — familiarity from Footprinting & Scanning course → [[Port-Scanning-Nmap]]

---

## Learning Objectives

1. **Perform comprehensive network enumeration with Nmap**
   - Identify live hosts, open ports, and services on the target network

2. **Effectively leverage the Nmap Scripting Engine (NSE)** → [[NSE]]
   - Automated and custom tasks: vulnerability detection, service version detection, advanced recon

3. **Analyse and interpret Nmap results**
   - Extract actionable information for penetration testing
   - Identify potential attack vectors for the exploitation phase

4. **Incorporate Nmap + NSE into the full pentesting lifecycle**
   - From initial recon through to post-exploitation activities
