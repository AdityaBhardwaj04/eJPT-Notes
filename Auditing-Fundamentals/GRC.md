# Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

**Related:** [[Common-Standards-Frameworks-Guidelines]] | [[Security-Auditing-and-Penetration-Testing]] | [[00-INDEX]]

---

## What is GRC?

**GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance)** is a comprehensive framework used by organisations to manage and align their:
- **Governance** practices
- **Risk management** strategies
- **Compliance** with regulatory requirements

This holistic approach helps organisations maintain **transparency, accountability, and resilience** in an increasingly complex regulatory environment.

---

## The Three Components

### 1. Governance
> The framework of policies, procedures, and practices ensuring an organisation achieves its objectives, manages risks, and complies with legal/regulatory requirements.

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Policy Development** | Creating clear, comprehensive security policies |
| **Roles & Responsibilities** | Defining who is accountable for security management |
| **Accountability** | Establishing accountability mechanisms for security performance |

### 2. Risk Management
> Identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks that could negatively impact an organisation's assets or operations.

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Risk Identification** | Recognising potential threats and vulnerabilities |
| **Risk Assessment** | Evaluating the likelihood and impact of identified risks |
| **Risk Mitigation** | Implementing measures to reduce or eliminate risks |

Formula: **Risk = Likelihood × Impact**

### 3. Compliance
> Ensuring the organisation adheres to relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Regulatory Requirements** | Meeting legal obligations — GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS |
| **Internal Policies** | Adhering to the organisation's own security policies and procedures |
| **Audits & Assessments** | Conducting regular reviews to verify compliance |

---

## Why GRC Matters for Penetration Testers

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Comprehensive Assessment** | Understanding GRC helps conduct more thorough and relevant assessments |
| **Enhanced Reporting** | Allows you to frame findings in the context of organisational policies, risk management, and compliance — in language the client understands |
| **Strategic Recommendations** | Enables more strategic recommendations that align with the organisation's GRC framework |

> If you understand the GRC framework a client uses, you can **present your report in their language** — making it far more actionable and valuable to them.

---

## GRC in Practice

Most medium to large organisations have a GRC function. When you're engaged as a pen tester:
- The audit report you receive will be framed within their GRC context
- Your report should reference the relevant compliance standards (PCI DSS, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Risk ratings should align with the organisation's risk methodology
- Recommendations should be practical within their governance structure

---

## Key Takeaways

- GRC = the umbrella framework that ties governance, risk, and compliance together
- As a pen tester, you'll mostly deal with **compliance** — but knowing governance and risk management context makes you a better consultant
- Understanding GRC allows you to **tailor your report** to what the client actually needs to hear
- The most relevant regulations in GRC for pen testers: **PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, ISO 27001**
